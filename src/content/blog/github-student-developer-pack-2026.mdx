The **GitHub Student Developer Pack** bundles free access to developer tools for as long as you're a student. Our video walkthrough on this is the most-watched on the channel, so here's the written version you can follow step by step.

<Callout>
Offers inside the pack change over time. Always check the live list at [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack).
</Callout>

## 1. Check you're eligible

- You're currently enrolled in a degree or diploma-granting course (college, university or similar).
- You have a GitHub account. Free is fine.
- You can prove you're a student: a college email address, a student ID card with a valid date, or an official enrolment document.

## 2. Set up your GitHub account first

Most rejections happen because the account isn't ready. Before you apply:

1. **Add your college email** under *Settings → Emails* and verify it, if your college gives you one.
2. **Turn on two-factor authentication** under *Settings → Password and authentication*.
3. **Add your full legal name** to your profile and billing information. It must match the name on your ID exactly.

## 3. Apply

1. Go to [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack) and click **Sign up for Student Developer Pack**.
2. Choose **Student**, then select your college from the list.
3. Allow location access if asked, and **switch off any VPN**. GitHub checks that you're near your college.
4. Upload your proof. Use your camera to capture the ID clearly: name, college name and a valid date must be readable.
5. Submit, and wait. Approval can take anywhere from a few minutes to a few days.

## 4. If you get rejected

Read the rejection email. It says why. The usual fixes:

- **Name mismatch**: update your GitHub billing name to match your ID exactly.
- **Document unreadable or missing a date**: retake the photo in good light, or use a fee receipt / bonafide certificate that shows the current academic year.
- **Location**: turn off your VPN and apply from (or near) campus.
- **College not listed**: add it manually. It may take longer to review.

You can reapply as soon as you've fixed the issue.

## 5. Claim your benefits

Once approved, benefits can take up to a few days to activate. Then open the pack page and claim offers one by one. Highlights usually include:

- **GitHub Copilot**, free for verified students. See our <VideoLink id="O_P4OJcrgRc" title="GitHub Copilot in VS Code (FREE) + Developer Coupons Explained">Copilot in VS Code video</VideoLink>.
- **GitHub Pro** and extra Codespaces hours.
- A **free domain** for a year from partner registrars, perfect for your portfolio.
- **Cloud credits** and pro IDEs from partner companies.
